Who we are

Glow Power Building Contracting is a Dubai fit-out contractor. We turn empty units into offices, shops and restaurants that are ready to open.

Our name comes from what we care about most: how a space feels when the lights come on. Our site team includes carpenters, gypsum fixers, electricians and painters who work to one programme under one project manager.

We work directly with business owners, and alongside interior designers who need a contractor to deliver their drawings accurately.

What clients get from us

One programme

Every trade on one schedule, shared with you each week.

Fixed price

A priced scope before work starts, with variations agreed in writing.

Light tested

We set up and test lighting scenes with you before handover.
