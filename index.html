Why lighting leads

Light is the first thing your visitors notice.

A fit-out is judged in the first five seconds: how the reception glows, how the shelves read, how warm the meeting room feels.

We plan the ceilings, the joinery and the lighting as one drawing, so they work together on day one.

From our base in International City we deliver complete fit-outs, from authority drawings and partitions to finishing, furniture installation and handover.
