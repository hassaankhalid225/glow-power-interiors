From empty unit to opening day

A typical office fit-out runs through five stages.

  1. Survey and brief

    We measure the unit and agree what each space needs to do.

  2. Design and approvals

    Layouts, ceiling and lighting plans, and authority submissions.

  3. Build

    Partitions, ceilings, MEP adjustments, joinery and flooring.

  4. Light and finish

    Lighting scenes, paint, glass and furniture installation.

  5. Handover

    Snagging, completion certificate and maintenance contacts.
